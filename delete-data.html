This page explains how users of Cash Arrow Pro – Play & Earn (developer: Cash Arrow Pro) can ask for their data to be deleted. The App has no user accounts or login; your data is linked to the User ID shown in the App.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the App and tap the Settings (gear) button. Note the User ID shown at the top.
  2. Send an email to growesports.com@gmail.com with the subject "Cash Arrow Pro – Delete my data" and include your User ID. If you made a withdrawal request, also include the email address you entered in it.
  3. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All withdrawal requests linked to your User ID, including the name, email address and payout method you entered.
  • The anonymous sign-in account linked to your device (these are also removed automatically after 30 days).
  • Analytics data associated with your app instance, where Google Analytics allows deletion.

What may be kept

  • Records of payouts that were already paid, kept only as long as required for legal, tax or accounting purposes (and for fraud prevention), after which they are deleted.
  • Aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

Data on your device

Your game progress and in-game balance are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Cash Arrow Pro → Storage → Clear data). Note that this also removes your in-game balance.
