This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Cash Arrow Pro – Play & Earn (the "App", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who can use the App

The App is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

  • Game data stored on your device: your level, in-game balance, hints, tasks and settings (sound, vibration). This stays on your device unless stated below.
  • App identifiers: a random User ID created by the App on your device, and an anonymous sign-in identifier from Google Firebase Authentication. These do not contain your name or contact details.
  • Withdrawal information (only if you choose to request a withdrawal): the payout method you select, your email address, your name, the amount requested, your User ID, the number of levels you have cleared and the App version. This is needed to review and pay your request.
  • Usage and diagnostics: through Google Firebase Analytics we receive information such as levels started and completed, buttons used, app version, device model, operating system, approximate country and app-instance identifiers. This helps us understand how the App is used and fix problems.
  • Advertising data: the App may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location, phone number or payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress.
  • To review, verify and pay withdrawal requests, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To understand usage, improve the App and fix bugs.
  • To show and measure advertising.
  • To reply when you contact us.

4. Third-party services

The App uses services provided by Google LLC, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/privacy

When we pay a withdrawal we send the payment to the account you provided using the payment service you selected. That service handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, when needed to make a payout you requested, or when required by law.

6. Storage, security and retention

Withdrawal requests are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and can only be created by the App; they cannot be read by other users. We keep withdrawal records for as long as needed to process payouts, handle disputes and meet legal or accounting obligations. Anonymous sign-in accounts are automatically removed after 30 days. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: email us at growesports.com@gmail.com with your User ID (shown in the App under Settings) and we will delete your withdrawal records and associated data, except where we must keep it by law. Full steps: Delete My Data.
  • Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Ads personalisation: you can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

8. International users

The App is available worldwide. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including on Google's servers. Where local law gives you additional rights (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may exercise them by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
